The Woman Behind the World

Meet Original Hippie

I created Girlz Run the World because I believe too many women are waiting for permission to live the life they want. This is your sign. You don't need it.

The truth is — I didn't grow up traveling. I didn't have a passport full of stamps or family vacations around the world. In fact, I didn't take my first plane ride until I was 24 years old.

Before that, the world felt much smaller.

What started as one trip turned into a passion for exploring, learning, and challenging myself. Since then, I've traveled across multiple countries, lived abroad in China, worked in aviation, and experienced cultures, people, and perspectives that completely changed the way I see the world.

It made me uncomfortable.

Travel gave me something far more valuable than photos and stamps. It forced me to navigate cities where I didn't speak the language. It taught me how to solve problems when things didn't go as planned. It taught me confidence, independence, resilience, and how to trust myself.

Every trip pushed me outside of my comfort zone and helped me become a stronger version of who I already was. Travel exposed me to new ideas, new ways of living, and possibilities I never knew existed. It reminded me that there is so much more waiting for us on the other side of fear.

The Mission

"Girlz Run the World isn't just about vacations."

It's about helping women stop procrastinating on their dreams. It's about realizing you don't have to have everything figured out before you start.

It's about choosing yourself, taking the trip, booking the flight, trying something new, and discovering what you're capable of along the way.

Whether you're planning your first solo adventure, a girls getaway, a birthday trip, or simply looking for the confidence to finally take the leap — I'm here to help make the process easier.

I believe meaningful travel doesn't have to be expensive, luxurious, or perfect. Sometimes all it takes is the courage to begin.

So here's your reminder: the world is waiting for you.

Get up. Get out. Go girl.
